**Missing-data rule (fixed before computing).** A held-out window is covered for a rule when it holds at least one timed record of the rule's source. A pair is assessable for false positives only if at least 30 of its 60 held-out windows are covered (half the held-out hour). Below that it is *not adequately assessable*: neither a success nor a failure of the rule. False positives are computed on covered windows only, with the Wilson 95% upper bound. A rule of type "none" cannot alert, so its 0/60 is *not applicable*.
